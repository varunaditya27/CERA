**Status: working research prototype; scientific evaluation and final deliverables remain incomplete.** This report brings together the current implementation and results as of 17 November 2026. It does not replace the [decision and work log](../history/work_log.md), [completion evidence review](completion_review.md), or individual study records.

## The problem and the approach

A protein variant is a modified amino-acid sequence. Measuring its activity or stability in a laboratory can be expensive, so a model predicts scores and a search chooses which variants to measure next. CERA investigates whether the search can detect warning signs and safely change its scoring rule when predictions and observations disagree.

The prototype uses previously measured datasets to simulate measurements. An optional [Amylase development policy](../studies/repair/reactive_calibration.md#findings) updates range-setting examples after a coverage warning; it is not part of the completed evaluation studies and has no adaptive-coverage guarantee. It converts sequences into numerical representations with a frozen ESM-2 model, fits a group of five regression models (or an experimental Amylase-only group with three ridge models and two neural networks), calculates prediction ranges, and selects candidates using predicted fitness, uncertainty, distance from known variants and batch diversity. It records measurements, warnings, proposed changes, checks and stopping. Gemini can propose bounded weights or prioritize a fixed repair menu; application code validates proposed changes. Raw source files remain accessible to someone with file access: the measurement interface prevents accidental disclosure through its API, not adversarial access.

## What the completed studies show

- **Four-protein, unrestricted starts:** 360 runs completed. Against fixed reward at each method's actual stop, each repair policy had 0 higher, 35 tied and 5 lower best scores across 40 paired starts. Rule-based repair stopped after failed checks in 35 runs and Gemini-assisted repair in 36. Fixed reward used its full 100-measurement budget in all runs. [Results and audit](../studies/multiprotein/four_protein_findings.md).
- **IRED, low-change starts:** 90 runs completed. Each repair policy was higher in one pair, tied in seven and lower in two; both stopped after failed repairs in all ten runs. Each used 410 new measurements in total versus 1,000 for fixed reward. The starting pool included eligible validation-marked train rows, and later candidates came from the entire eligible pool; this is not an official split comparison. [Results and audit](../studies/multiprotein/ired_low_change.md#findings).
- **Hydro, low-fitness starts:** 270 runs completed. Each repair policy was lower in 25 of 30 pairs and tied in five at its actual stop. Each used 1,130 new measurements versus 3,000 for fixed reward; 27 of 30 runs stopped after failed checks. A later comparison at the same measurement count gave 30 ties per repair policy. The starting pool uses the benchmark's score-derived membership, and later candidate access is unrestricted. [Results and audits](../studies/multiprotein/hydro_low_fitness.md#findings).
- **Repair without early stopping (exploratory, post hoc):** repeating the IRED and Hydro starts with a repair arm that never stops early, every run used the full 100-measurement budget, and repair never scored below fixed reward in 40 starts (38 ties, 2 higher). It scored higher than normal repair in 28 starts and lower in none. The earlier deficits therefore come from stopping early, not from the repaired reward; the study does not show that repair helps. [Protocol and findings](../studies/repair/repair_without_stopping.md).
- **Held-out static prediction:** Median range coverage was 90.0% for IRED and 0.0%, 11.7% and 10.6% for the three Hydro proteins. These are medians over ten starts, not formal coverage guarantees. The Hydro split is score-derived and the study excludes variants above four substitutions. [Results and audit](../studies/hybrid/heldout_shift_prediction.md#findings).
- **Amylase position shift:** The five-ridge predictor had 90.2% median coverage and median rank correlation of 0.0005 across ten starts on 1,709 eligible far-side variants. This covers static prediction on development data. [Position-shift findings](../studies/amylase/position_shift.md)

The above starts reuse fixed protein datasets. They are not independent biological replications. Comparisons at actual stop include different measurement counts and do not isolate the effect of changing the reward from the effect of stopping. Equal best scores do not prove the policies are equivalent.

## Why repairs stopped

In the Hydro low-fitness study, both repair policies stopped after consecutive failed actions in different batches in all 54 early-stopped policy runs. Across both policies, 357 proposed changes were rejected. Every rejected proposal failed either the check that all flagged candidates disappeared in a replay or the direct diversity-resolution check. Some also failed ranking preservation. These are properties of the implemented checks, not proof that the rejected or replacement candidates would be better. The stored calibration check uses existing intervals and cannot itself show that a changed reward improves future coverage. [Diagnosis and audit](../studies/multiprotein/hydro_repair_stopping.md#findings).

## What is implemented and how to run it

- The web workspace is in `frontend/` and `backend/app/api/`. It shows dataset information and the pinned mixed-predictor development comparison, and predicts eligible Amylase sequences using the exported development model and verified local representation model; it also runs bounded local experiments across Amylase, IRED and three hydrophobic-core proteins using fixed reward, rule-based repair or optional Gemini-assisted repair. The runner offers the experimental mixed predictor for Amylase; other datasets use five ridge members. Completed local runs have a batch-by-batch review and an optional [decision check](../workspace/local_decision_audit.md) based on recorded predictions.
- The experimental pipeline is in `backend/app/experiments/`, `predictors/`, `features/`, `rewards/`, `llm/`, `analysis/` and `provenance/`. It runs from scripts and writes local records.
- `scripts/amylase/compile_reward_goal.py` can accept optional user goal text through `--goal`. The text is sent to Gemini and saved. The model can misunderstand it; review the bounded specification before use. The only supported objective is maximizing the dataset fitness score. [Goal compiler](../workspace/goal_compiler.md#natural-language-goals).
- `scripts/amylase/run_reward_pilot.py` consumes a saved specification. `--repair` enables rule-based repair, and `--gemini-repair` lets Gemini select among permitted repairs. The [README](../../README.md) covers setup and study commands.

Verification in this checkout includes 193 passing backend tests, a production frontend build, HTTP checks against a live local backend, and a live Gemini goal-to-Amylase episode. The local RTX 5050 is visible to PyTorch; the existing sequence representations were generated using the GPU. A fresh offline GPU check reproduced finite 320-value vectors, with a maximum padding difference of 6.56e-7. The [mixed predictor GPU pilot](../studies/hybrid/hybrid_predictor.md#findings) was functionally audited; its exported model has an [exploratory Amylase prediction comparison](../studies/hybrid/exported_model_evaluation.md#findings), and an [exploratory matched search comparison](../studies/hybrid/mixed_search_comparison.md#findings) has been replay-audited. A subsequent [exploratory static comparison](../studies/hybrid/mixed_heldout.md#findings) evaluated the mixed predictor on held-out IRED and Hydro variants; it did not evaluate mixed-predictor search or repair on those proteins. A larger embedding model has not been evaluated, and no claim is made that it would improve results. The fixed-reward demo passed a Chromium keyboard walkthrough and a fresh CPU install. The primary study decision audit checked all 360 saved runs and the regenerated evaluation report matched the committed copy byte for byte. [Submission verification](submission_readiness.md)

## Evidence that remains missing

1. A test-only sequential shift study and independent biological validation of the warning types. The existing static split check and unrestricted searches answer different questions.
2. Stronger evidence about reverting an accepted reward: the [later-rollback development check](../studies/repair/later_rollback.md#findings) implemented and tested the mechanism, but no real run triggered it. Some repair-removal and universal-penalty comparisons also did not exercise their interventions.
3. An independent causal estimate of repair benefit or proof that the proposed after-repair coverage and regret targets were met. The [repair-without-stopping study](../studies/repair/repair_without_stopping.md) separates repair from stopping in exploratory form but shows no benefit beyond fixed reward. Some runs stop before enough later observations are available.
4. Independent evaluation of the mixed predictor beyond the [matched development searches](../studies/hybrid/mixed_search_comparison.md#findings) and its [post-episode development model](../studies/hybrid/model_export.md#findings). A [report for review](../../report/CERA_final_report.pdf) and [project review presentation](../../presentation/project_review.html) summarize current results and limitations; the workshop draft is available for guide review, and institutional formatting remains unconfirmed. The web runner supports only local simulations with the default or a reviewed bounded goal specification and is not a complete experiment-management interface.

The [completion evidence review](completion_review.md) provides the detailed requirement-by-requirement limitations. Provider token counts in study records are not verified billing. Larger models, new tests and new methods must be evaluated without using evaluation outcomes to tune the procedure and without presenting exploratory findings as confirmation.
