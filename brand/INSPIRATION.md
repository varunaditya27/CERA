## Design diagnosis of previous branch

The first revision was better structured than the original dashboard but missed the emotional entry point. Its three-rail diamond mark was not an ownable identity. Newsreader made it resemble many generative editorial layouts. The fitness wireframe was not connected enough to a decision. Most pages described the research rather than helping visitors test their understanding.

## Correction made

- Rebuilt the identity around an open C and a measurement mark.
- Moved to Geologica/Onest/Fragment Mono and explicit typography axes.
- Promoted an interactive explanatory example to a first-class route, with presentation controls.
- Provided the real comparator on Results and a code-level System map.
- Kept the **real** amylase results and the **illustrative** example clearly distinct.

## Verification boundary

Inspiration sources inform design choices; they do not validate the CERA science. Browser screenshot comparison and a Next production build remain separate QA gates. Do not present these as complete merely because the code has been committed.
